## The set gets tired as the record runs out

The tube has had two faults since the beginning: a soft band that walks down the
screen every several seconds, and the wordmark losing its line for a tenth of a
second every minute or so. Neither of them has ever known what was on the deck.

Now both do. The gaps between them close up as the needle runs into the record,
so a set is at its steadiest on the lead-in and at its most restless in the
run-out — the same two events, arriving at the end of forty minutes rather than
at the start of them.

**Nothing new happens and nothing gets bigger.** The tear is the same size it
always was, the band falls at the same speed, and the shortest gap the tube can
have is the one it could always have had: what stops happening is the long
quiet stretches. It is squared as well, so the first side of a record is very
nearly untouched and the tiring is done in the last quarter — a set that was
already restless four tracks in would be a set that was tired when you put the
record on.

It resets when a record does, which puts it on the same moment as the strike:
the tube is degaussed back to its freshest at the instant something new goes on.

Held still by Reduce Motion and by `MUTHUR_CRT=0`, because it is the two faults
those already govern, and not a third thing.

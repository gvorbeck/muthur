Three things on the playing screen that were still pictures and are now
instruments. Nothing about playback, importing or burning changed.

## The tube strikes when a record goes on

A set that has just been handed something to draw does not simply have the
picture appear on it — the degauss coil fires, and the geometry shivers and
settles. That now happens, once, at the moment a record arrives: under a second
of the raster losing its width and getting it back, with the phosphor coming up
alongside it.

It is an **event**, which is the whole reason it is allowed to exist. You cannot
see it a second time without putting another record on, so it can never become
the thing you are watching instead of the track list. The music is already
playing underneath it either way — nothing waits on the picture.

Held still by Reduce Motion and by `MUTHUR_CRT=0`.

## The track number stops snapping

`TRACK 04 OF 11` becoming `TRACK 05 OF 11` is the one mechanical event in a
gapless record — the music deliberately gives you nothing at that moment. The
readout used to throw that away by snapping. Now the old figure stays faintly on
the glass while the new one strikes, the way a phosphor and a seven-segment
display both actually behave.

Not a cross-fade: the new figure arrives in a fifth of a second and the old one
takes more than twice that to go, so the two readings are never equally legible
at once.

Held still by Reduce Motion. **Not** by `MUTHUR_CRT` — that variable turns off
the two *faults* (the falling band, the tearing wordmark), and this is the tube
working correctly rather than failing.

## There is a needle in the run-out

The lead-out under the track list — grooves tightening to a dead groove — has
been a fixed picture since it was drawn. Through the last track, and only then,
there is now a needle in it: a lit groove with a stylus sitting in it, running
down the field and reaching the dead groove exactly as the music stops.

It rides the grooves that are actually drawn, which means it accelerates on its
own the way a needle does — equal time per groove against a pitch that is
closing. When the record finishes it stays in the dead groove rather than
disappearing, because that is where a needle goes.

Gated on nothing at all, including Reduce Motion. It is a reading, like the two
meters below it, and a meter that stopped under Reduce Motion would be a broken
meter rather than a considerate one.

## Known

Nothing new. Parity stands where it did — a terminal has no raster to degauss,
no phosphor to decay and no room under the last track, so none of this is
something either bash script has an opinion on.
